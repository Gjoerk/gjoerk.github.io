Room

Controls

Mouse
Left-click or drag selects · double-click selects all of that kind on screen · Shift adds
Right-click moves, gathers, builds, repairs or attacks · Ctrl + right-click attack-moves
Right-click with a building selected sets where its new units gather
Mouse wheel zooms · cursor at the window edge, WASD or arrows scroll
Minimap: left-click or drag to look there, right-click to send the selected units there
Touch
Tap selects · with units selected, tap the map, a resource or an enemy to send them there
Drag scrolls the map · pinch zooms · “Select area” lets you drag a box · “Deselect” lets go
Press and hold to look at something without giving an order
Keys
X stop · Delete destroys your selection · Shift + 1–9 stores a group, 1–9 selects it
. next idle villager · , all idle soldiers · Space jumps to the last attack on you
Enter chat · F fullscreen · M sound · H or Esc closes this
Good to know
Build a mill, lumber camp or mining camp next to food, wood, gold or stone so villagers walk less.
Walls are dragged out as a line and block everyone; a gate lets you and your allies through.
Right-click the castle with your king to shelter him. Lose your king and you are out.
