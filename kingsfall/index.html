Controls

Mouse
Left-click or drag selects · double-click selects all of that kind on screen · Shift adds
Right-click moves, gathers, builds, repairs or attacks · Ctrl + right-click attack-moves
Right-click with a building selected sets where its new units gather
Mouse wheel zooms · cursor at the window edge, WASD or arrows scroll
Minimap: left-click or drag to look there, right-click to send the selected units there
Touch
Tap selects · with units selected, tap the map, a resource or an enemy to send them there
Drag scrolls the map · pinch zooms · “Select area” lets you drag a box · “Deselect” lets go
Press and hold to look at something without giving an order
Keys
X stop · Delete destroys your selection · Shift + 1–9 stores a group, 1–9 selects it
. next idle villager (double-click its button for all of them) · , all troops · Space jumps to the last attack on you
T gives the selected villagers the same job as the villager you gave an order last; if that was a new building, you place another of it
The bell at the town centre calls every villager into shelter; ring it again to send them back to work
Shift while placing buildings puts them on the villagers' list: they build one after the other
Groups walk in formation at the pace of their slowest member; fields can be walked over.
Soldiers are Aggressive (attack enemy units they see) or Defensive (fight back only when hit); they attack buildings only when told to
Enter chat · F fullscreen (the cursor stays on the screen; Esc frees it) · M sound · H or Esc closes this
Good to know
One relic lies somewhere far from the towns. Only monks can carry it: right-click it with a monk, and he takes it to your monastery. While you keep it there, all your troops and towers get +2 attack. It falls where the monk dies or the monastery is destroyed, so it can be taken from you
Build a mill, lumber camp or mining camp next to food, wood, gold or stone so villagers walk less.
Walls are dragged out as a line and block everyone; a gate lets you and your allies through.
Right-click the town centre or castle with your king, or with empty-handed villagers, to shelter them inside; a flag shows someone is in.
Monks (from the monastery) heal friends on their own; right-click an enemy unit with a monk to win it over.
Lose your king and you are out. The population limit is set by the host before the game.
